Green Crackers,
Clear Rules

Karnataka’s two-hour window—and what “green” actually promises.

India UnpackedKarnatakaScience + everyday choicesMyth vs fact

ABOUT 4 MINUTES

01

First, watch the clock

8 pm–10 pm

Karnataka · 8, 9 & 10 November 2026
Only green crackers permitted. Follow additional local restrictions.

That includes tonight, Monday, 9 November. These dates and hours come from reports of Karnataka’s Deepavali directions. [1]

The two-hour window is a limit, not a target. You do not need to keep bursting crackers until the final minute.

ZappyWhat if the box isn’t finished by ten?

Prof. BoltThen the box isn’t finished. It’s Deepavali, not a homework deadline.

The timing applies even when the crackers carry a green label.
02

A permitted time is not every place

Karnataka Police guidance also restricts use in crowded and sensitive locations, including around hospitals and educational institutions. A permitted time does not make every place suitable. [2]

Check the directions for your area before choosing a spot. The practical question is both “When?” and “Where?”

A green label does not override restrictions on time or location.
THE DEEP DIVEWhat the label means—and what it leaves unsaid.
03

Meet NEERI: the science behind “green”

It has nothing to do with the colour of the sparks—or how much green appears on the packet.

CSIR-NEERI, the National Environmental Engineering Research Institute, is a national research institute in Nagpur. Its work includes developing firecracker formulations designed to release fewer pollutants than conventional versions. [3]

Scientists change ingredients or their proportions to reduce emissions. “Green” describes a tested improvement; it does not promise that burning the product has no environmental cost.

ZappyThe shopkeeper says these are environmentally friendly.

Prof. BoltAsk what makes them green. The adjective should have some paperwork.

04

Less pollution does not mean none

One important pollutant is particulate matter: tiny particles suspended in the air.

NEERI’s criteria include at least 30% lower particulate emissions than a conventional cracker of the same category. Another qualifying route combines at least 20% lower particulate emissions with 10% lower gaseous emissions. [3]

These are specific emissions comparisons. They do not mean that the neighbourhood’s air becomes 30% cleaner. Outdoor air has other pollution sources, and weather also affects what accumulates.

“Lower emissions” is an improvement. It does not mean smokeless, silent or harmless.
05

Quantity still matters

A lower-emission product can lose its advantage if we use much more of it.

Illustration: suppose one conventional cracker emits 100 units of particles, and an improved one emits 70. Two improved crackers emit 140 units—more than the single conventional cracker.

This is our illustrative arithmetic, not a measurement of a particular product. The lesson is that how many we use matters alongside emissions from each one.

ZappySo buying green doesn’t give me permission to burst twice as many?

Prof. BoltCorrect. The atmosphere counts the smoke, not your good intentions.

A cleaner individual product does not guarantee a cleaner evening.
06

Check the claim before paying

Look for the prescribed green-cracker identification logo and QR code. Government guidance describes checking these markings using the designated verification system. A green packet or a verbal assurance is not the same as verification. [4]

Buy from a licensed seller. Police guidance also calls for adult supervision for children, suitable fire-extinguishing material nearby, and distance from vehicles, electrical installations and combustible materials. [2]

Use the prescribed verification route. Do not treat the mere presence of a QR square as proof.
07

OPINION · Bolt’s verdict

BRIGHT SIDE

Reducing emissions per cracker is a useful technical improvement.

HARD PART

A reassuring label can be mistaken for permission to use more or ignore other restrictions.

MY TAKE

Choose verified products if using fireworks, keep the quantity modest and respect the time and place rules.

ZappyThe sensible purchase might be a smaller box, then?

Prof. BoltQuite possibly. Celebration isn’t measured by how much packaging is empty.

08

So what should Zappy do?

Before buying: use a licensed seller and check the prescribed markings and verification details.

Before lighting: confirm the time and local location restrictions. Arrange adult supervision and follow the product’s safety directions.

At ten: finish the fireworks. There is no need to finish the box.

Enjoy the festival while leaving room for everyone else to enjoy the evening too.
