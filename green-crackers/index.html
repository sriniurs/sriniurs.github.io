Green Crackers on Trial

It's the morning after Deepavali, and the Colony Court of Kuvempunagar is meeting in the park, ankle-deep in burnt paper. Five popular beliefs about "green" crackers are in the dock. Judge Bolt has had about two hours of sleep.

India UnpackedDeepavaliGreen CrackersMyth vs Fact
TODAY IS SPECIAL!It's National Legal Services Day (free legal help for people who can't afford a lawyer) and Govardhan Puja in many states ⚖️ Fitting, because today our crackers go on trial. In Delhi-NCR, the full cracker ban is back from this morning.

PART 1: THE CASE FILEThe rules, and where "green" came from

EP 1

Order! Order! The morning after

ORDER! ORDER!

ZappyYour Honour, I have brought the evidence. One full bag.

Prof. BoltZappy, that is my doormat. And it is still smoking.

The rules this year

Delhi (Supreme Court, 2 Nov): green crackers only, burst only on 7 and 8 Nov, in two short windows. From today, 9 Nov, a full ban again.

Karnataka: green only, 8 to 10 pm, 8 to 10 Nov. Get caught with banned stock and the whole warehouse is sealed.

Prof. BoltTwo hours a night. My neighbour started at 7:58 and called it a "warm-up".

PLOT TWIST

Government labs led by CSIR-NEERI launched green crackers in 2019, after the Supreme Court banned barium in 2018.

Barium is what gave old crackers their bright green colour. So the "green" cracker was born by banning the green chemical.

Prof. Bolt"Green" means kinder to the air, not the colour. Like Silk Board junction: no silk, only traffic.

PART 2: FIVE MYTHS IN THE DOCKEach belief takes the stand

EP 2

Myth 1: "Green crackers don't pollute"

ZappyMy uncle says green crackers are so clean, you could burst one in the pooja room.

Prof. BoltYour uncle also says he could have played for India. Next witness.

MYTHGreen means zero smoke, zero harm.

FACTNEERI's rule: at least 30% less fine dust than an ordinary cracker. So up to about 70% of the smoke is still there (our arithmetic).

And the smoke you can't see? A 2022 DTU–IIT Roorkee study found green crackers gave off a bigger share of ultrafine particles, small enough to reach deep into the lungs. One study, but a fair warning.

BUSTED

Cleaner, not clean. And here's the catch: the cut is per cracker. Burst twice as many and the air gets about 140% of the old smoke (our arithmetic).

ZappySo "green" made people feel less guilty, and they bought more?

Prof. BoltSame thing happens with "diet" sweets. Ask any Mysuru sweet shop.

EP 3

Myth 2: "Green crackers are quiet"

DHAM! DHAM!

The colony dog, Tiger, has filed a complaint. He has been under the cot since Saturday. His paw print is the signature.

MYTHGreen crackers barely make a sound.

FACTAbout 110–125 decibels, against about 160 for many old-style ones. The legal limit for one cracker is 125 dB; a quiet residential night is meant to be 45 dB.

Every extra 10 dB is about ten times the sound energy.

HALF TRUE

Prof. BoltQuieter than before. Not quiet enough for a sleeping baby, a dog, or a retired judge.

EP 4

Myth 3: "If the box says GREEN, it's green"

BEEP!

ZappyI scanned the QR code on my box. It opened... a saree sale.

Prof. BoltThen either it's a fake, or it's a very confused cracker.

MYTHThe word "green" on the box is proof.

FACTOn 14 Oct 2026, Mumbai's Awaaz Foundation tested 17 brands from licensed shops. All said "green". Some had no QR code; some had fake ones.

BUSTED

A real one carries the CSIR-NEERI green logo and a QR code that works in the official CSIR-NEERI Green QR Code app, showing the maker, licence and test details.

Myth 4: "Green crackers fixed Delhi's air"

ZappyDelhi allowed only green crackers last Diwali. Case closed?

Prof. BoltLet me read the evidence... *cough* ...I can't see the evidence.

Micrograms per cubic metre, CPCB data. WHO's 24-hour guideline is 15.

BUSTED

The first green-only Diwali in years gave Delhi its highest post-Diwali PM2.5 in five years. Too many crackers, fakes among them, and still winter air that traps smoke.

EP 6

Myth 5: "Crackers are as old as Deepavali"

ZappyWhen Lord Rama came home, everyone burst atom bombs, no?

Prof. BoltWith what? Gunpowder hadn't been invented. They lit lamps. Lovely, quiet lamps.

MYTHCrackers have always been part of Deepavali.

FACTGunpowder came from China, around the 9th century. India's earliest solid record of fireworks is from 1443, at the Vijayanagara court: Hampi, right here in Karnataka.

WAIT, REALLY?

Today about 90% of India's crackers come from Sivakasi, a town that started out making matches in the 1920s. Its old makers say the first bomb they ever produced was the Atom bomb.

ZappySo the Atom bomb is older than my grandfather?

Prof. BoltAnd louder.

THE KARNATAKA FILEWhat happened here last year

EP 7

Cleaner air, louder nights, and the people watching

Bengaluru, Deepavali 2025

Average AQI about 83, down from 120 the year before (KSPCB); rain and empty roads helped. But noise went up: Sarjapur averaged 90.4 dB.

Mysuru had 200+ cracker varieties on sale, against barely 20 thirty years ago.

MYTHIf you're not holding the match, you're safe.

FACTBengaluru's Minto Eye Hospital saw 37 cases in Deepavali 2025. 20 were bystanders. Narayana Nethralaya saw 93 in three days.

The usual culprits: atom bombs, bijli crackers and flowerpots.

ZappyI never light crackers. I just stand and watch. Totally safe.

Prof. BoltWatchers are about half the patients. Green or not, a cracker is still a small explosion. The chemistry changed. The physics didn't.

EP 8

OPINION: Judge Bolt's verdict, and what Zappy should do

ACQUITTED (PARTLY)

The green cracker is a real step forward: less dust, no barium, quieter.

The real culprits are the fakes and the quantity. A 30% cleaner cracker bought by the sackful still chokes the city.

Tonight is the last night in Karnataka

  • 📱 Scan the QR before you burst. No page? Bin it.
  • ⏰ 8–10 pm only. Fewer crackers, shorter show.
  • 👓 Glasses on, water bucket ready, step back.
  • 🚫 Never re-light a dud. Never bend over a lit one.
  • 👁️ Something in the eye? Don't rub. Rinse with clean water, go straight to an eye hospital.
MY TAKE

Fewer, genuine, shorter. Let the diya be the hero again. Celebrate loudly with sweets, not decibels.

ZappySo I can keep my sparklers, if they pass the QR test?

Prof. BoltCase dismissed. Now please sweep my doormat.
