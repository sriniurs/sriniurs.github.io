Knee Replacement

Ten illustrated patient articles on choosing knee replacement, recovering movement and living with your new knee.

  1. When to Consider Knee ReplacementHow Do I Know When It’s Time for a Knee Replacement?How symptoms, X-rays, treatment so far and the activities you want back shape the decision.
  2. Replacing Both KneesIf Both My Knees Hurt, Should I Replace Them Together or One at a Time?What changes when both knees are replaced together, how to weigh the risks and what recovery at home may look like.
  3. Knee Replacement ImplantsWhat Exactly Gets Replaced in a Total Knee Replacement?An illustrated look at the new joint surfaces, the plastic insert and what may happen to the kneecap.

  7. Knee Stiffness After SurgeryWhy Is It So Hard to Bend or Fully Straighten My New Knee?What limits knee movement after surgery, what the physiotherapist measures and when to ask for a review.
  8. Kneeling After SurgeryWill I Be Able to Kneel, Sit Cross-Legged, or Use a Floor Toilet Again?What floor activities ask of the knee and how to plan for prayer, family meals and bathroom access.
  9. Persistent Pain After SurgeryWhy Does My Knee Still Hurt Months After Replacement?How the team investigates pain that persists or returns, and what to bring to your appointment.
  10. Implant Lifespan and RevisionHow Long Will My New Knee Last, and What Happens If It Wears Out?How to read the numbers, when to get the knee checked and what a second operation may involve.

FOR PATIENT EDUCATION ONLY

These articles are intended to help you better understand your symptoms and the care advised after consultation and treatment by your doctor. They are not a guide to self-diagnosis or self-treatment.

Follow your treating specialist’s advice. Do not start or change medicines, exercises or other treatments based on these articles.
