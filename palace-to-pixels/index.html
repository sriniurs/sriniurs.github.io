Mysuru: Palace to Pixels

A comic about how a royal city of silk, sandalwood and Dasara is turning into Karnataka's "second IT city". A fresh Mysuru graduate asks, and an old-timer who has seen it all answers: what the kings built, what the expressway changed, and where Mysuru goes next.

PastPresentFutureLand & jobs

THE PASTThe kingdom that was ahead of its time

EP 1

The kings who built a model state

ZappyMysuru is just palaces and Dasara, no? Bengaluru got all the tech.

Prof. BoltArre! A hundred years ago, this kingdom was the most modern place in India. Bengaluru got its electricity from our power station.

Mysuru's firsts Under the Wadiyars and Dewan Sir M. Visvesvaraya:

  • 1902: Shivanasamudra hydro-power station, one of Asia's first.
  • 1912–16: Silk factory, Bank of Mysore, Sandal Soap factory and the University of Mysore.
  • 1930s: KRS dam on the Kaveri, which still waters the region.
ZAP!

Mysuru's kings planned ahead: power, water, banks and colleges first. Industry followed. Same recipe works today.

EP 2

Pensioners' paradise

After Independence The capital moved to Bengaluru. Mysuru slowed down, and liked it.

Wide roads, clean air, cheap houses, good schools. Retired officers, professors and army men settled here. Food scientists came to CFTRI. Factories grew in Hebbal, Hootagalli and Nanjangud.

Prof. BoltLife was slow. Silk, sandalwood, incense sticks, Dasara once a year. Our children studied here, then took the bus to Bengaluru for jobs. That was the Mysuru story for 50 years.

The talent was born here but earned elsewhere. Mysuru exported its children.

EP 3

The giant training campus

Early 2000s Infosys builds a huge training campus on the edge of Mysuru.

CLASS IN SESSION!

Lakhs of freshers from all over India learned to code here over the years, then left for Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad.

ZappySo Mysuru was the school, and Bengaluru was the office.

Prof. BoltExactly. Small IT firms came too, but the big money never stayed. Mysuru was a training ground, not a headquarters.

THE PRESENTThe expressway changed everything

EP 4

3 hours became 90 minutes

March 2023 The 10-lane Bengaluru–Mysuru Expressway opens. About 118 km.

~3 hrs→~90 min
VROOOM!

Who won

  • Land along the Mysore Road belt (Bidadi, Ramanagara, Mandya) jumped.
  • Bengaluru families bought second homes and retirement flats here.
  • MUDA raised guidance values 6–10% in Vijayanagar, Kuvempunagar and Gokulam in early 2026.

Who lost

Highway eateries in Maddur and Channapatna that lived on slow traffic. And hotels: tourists now see the palace and drive back the same evening.

A fast road works both ways. It can bring jobs to Mysuru, or take Mysuru's people to Bengaluru every day.

EP 6

Palace, zoo and Dasara crowds

~50 lakhvisitors a year

About 35–40 lakh of them go to the palace and the zoo. During Dasara the city overflows.

Cleanest city, then not

India's cleanest city (2015, 2016)→Slipped to 27th (2023)

More people, more tourists, same old garbage system.

Tourism is Mysuru's oldest income, but most of it is day trips. Night stays, food and shopping are where the real money is.

EP 7

The airport that waits

Runway: 1,740 m Only small 72-seat ATR planes can land.

~92,000passengers in FY26

Down 3.3% from the year before. Bengaluru airport handles that in a few hours.

The plan

1,740 m→2,300 m (phase 1)→2,750 m (phase 2)

Land acquisition finished in early 2026. Then A320 and Boeing 737 jets can land, and direct flights to metros become possible.

ZappyWhy does a foreign company care about our runway?

Prof. BoltBecause the boss flying in from Frankfurt won't take a 3-hour cab from Bengaluru airport every visit. No jets, no big offices.

EP 8

Growing pains

When a quiet city gets busy

More people→New layouts on the edges→Traffic on Hunsur and Bannur roads→Old markets and heritage buildings decaying→Water and garbage strain

Old Mysuru is the charm. New Mysuru is the money. The city has to protect one while building the other.

THE FUTUREThe state's plan, and Prof. Bolt's guesses

EP 9

The "second IT city" plan

Target

$10 bndigital economy by 2030

Vision set by KDEM, the state's Beyond Bengaluru mission. Focus: IT, GCCs, electronics design and manufacturing, startups.

Sweets for companies outside Bengaluru

  • 100% electricity duty refunded for 5 years.
  • 30% property tax refunded for 3 years.
  • Higher patent and certification refunds than in Bengaluru.
  • A "Global Innovation District" tech park planned for Mysuru.
  • A regional campus of the state's new AI University.

Prof. BoltOffice rent here is a fraction of Bengaluru. Staff don't sit 2 hours in traffic. Attrition is lower because people want to stay home. That's the real pitch.

EP 10

Bedroom or boardroom?

ZZZ…

Bedroom town: people live in Mysuru, work in Bengaluru, spend in Bengaluru. Property goes up, local jobs don't.

KA-CHING!

Boardroom town: companies open real offices here. Salaries are spent here, in local shops, hospitals and schools.

Watch one number: how many people work in Mysuru, not just live here. That decides which Mysuru we get.

THE DEEP DIVEThe research behind the headlines

EP 11

The 1902 blueprint, in numbers

Shivanasamudra, the project that powered a kingdom

  • 1899: Dewan K. Seshadri Iyer approves the plan after an engineer sees Niagara Falls power.
  • ~19 months: erection finished by March 1902, a speed historians say would be hard to match today.
  • 30 June 1902: power reaches Kolar Gold Fields, about 150 km away.
  • 1905: power and street lights reach Bengaluru.

What cheap power built

Shivanasamudra + Shimsha power→Bhadravati iron & steel→Paper, sugar, textile mills→Aircraft factory

Also: University of Mysore (1916), the first university in Karnataka; Bank of Mysore; Sandal Soap.

Prof. BoltThe secret wasn't money. It was order: power first, then water, then colleges, then industry. Mysore was called one of the best-administered states anywhere. Today's "second IT city" plan is trying the same recipe in a different order.

The tier-2 wave is real, but small

5%→7%

Karnataka's lopsided map

In 2020, Bengaluru alone made about 95% of the state's IT exports. Every other city together: 5%.

Mysuru doesn't need to beat Bengaluru. It needs to beat Kochi and Coimbatore for the next mid-size office.

The government sweeteners

  • 100% electricity duty refunded for 5 years.
  • 30% property tax refunded for 3 years.
  • Up to 80% (₹8 lakh) certification costs refunded, vs 50% in Bengaluru.

ZappySo why isn't everyone moving already?

Prof. BoltBecause companies follow talent, not rent. Bengaluru has lakhs of experienced engineers; Mysuru has thousands. Cheaper rent saves maybe a tenth of an office's cost. Salaries are most of it. Mysuru wins when people want to move here, and many do.

Water: the hidden ceiling

Supply vs need (million litres a day)

Supplied (KRS + Kabini)
~240
Needed by 2025 (MUDA)

Most city water comes from the KRS dam on the Kaveri; about 60 MLD from the Kabini.

Leaks and losses

66%

"Non-revenue water" in an AMRUT city factsheet: water pumped but never billed, lost to leaks, theft and broken meters.

2026 so far

  • May: KRS at 81.8 ft against a 124.8 ft maximum.
  • Aug: Kabini supply to about 22 layouts suspended for nearly two months; outskirts on tankers.
  • The ₹595-crore Hale Unduwadi project, meant to add up to 300 MLD, is delayed.

Bengaluru ran dry in 2024. Mysuru draws from the same river. A "second IT city" with tanker-fed outskirts is a plan with a crack in it. Fixing leaks is the cheapest new water there is.

EP 16

Mysuru 2035: three endings

BOOM!

The good ending. Jets land, GCCs open mid-size centres, the AI campus feeds talent. Mysuru becomes what Pune is to Mumbai: its own city, not a suburb. Heritage core protected, tourism stays overnight.

HMM…

The likely ending. Steady IT growth, lots of remote and hybrid workers, property up strongly on the Bengaluru side of town. Mysuru gets busier but keeps some of its calm.

CRASH!

The bad ending. Airport delays drag on, AI shrinks IT hiring, Mysuru becomes a weekend-and-retirement town. Land prices run ahead of jobs, and the traffic arrives without the salaries.

Prof. BoltThese are my guesses, not forecasts. But Mysuru has one thing Bengaluru lost: people actually want to live here. If we don't spoil that, the jobs will come to us.

EP 17

So what should Zappy do?

Mysuru's other engines Not everything is IT.

  • Healthcare: patients come from Kodagu, Chamarajanagar, Mandya and across the Kerala and Tamil Nadu borders.
  • Education: university, medical and engineering colleges.
  • Tourism: heritage, wellness, yoga, food.
  • Food and manufacturing: CFTRI, electronics, auto parts.

ZappyShould I just go to Bengaluru like everyone else?

Prof. BoltLearn the skills Bengaluru pays for: AI, data, design. Then look for remote, hybrid or GCC jobs here. Bengaluru salary, Mysuru rent. That's the jackpot.

Buying land? Check the khata and approvals carefully. Plots near the city edges are being sold on "IT city" hype long before the IT jobs arrive.

THE END

Fridge-door cheat sheet

Past

  • Wadiyars built power, dams, banks and colleges first.
  • Then: pensioners' paradise, silk, sandal, Dasara.
  • Infosys trained India's coders here, but jobs went elsewhere.

Present

  • Expressway: Bengaluru in ~90 min. Land prices up.
  • 30,000+ IT staff, ~₹4,000 crore exports.
  • ~50 lakh tourists a year, mostly day trips.
  • Airport still only for small planes.

Deep dive

  • Shivanasamudra built in ~19 months; power first, industry later.
  • Mysuru ~₹4,000 cr IT exports; Kochi Infopark ~72,000 staff.
  • Homes ~half Bengaluru's price; talent, not rent, decides moves.
  • Water: ~240 MLD supplied vs 494 MLD needed; 66% lost in one study.
  • Dasara: 16 lakh visitors, ₹500 cr, but weekday hotels 30–50% full.

Future

  • Officially Karnataka's "second IT city".
  • $10 bn digital economy target by 2030.
  • Runway expansion is the key unlock.
  • Bedroom or boardroom: watch local jobs, not land prices.
