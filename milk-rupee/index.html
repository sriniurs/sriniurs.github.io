Where Does Your ₹54 Go?

On 1 November, Zappy's blue Nandini packet went from ₹46 to ₹54. He wants to know who got his ₹8. Prof. Bolt starts up his old TVS XL and offers him a "reverse milk run": from the milk booth in Mysuru, all the way back to the cow.

Money TalksMilk MathsBooth to CowYour Wallet
TODAY IS SPECIAL!It's National Education Day, the birthday of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, independent India's first Education Minister 📚 So today we have a short class in milk maths. No exam, and you can bring your coffee.

PART 1: THE HIKEWhat changed on 1 November

EP 1

The packet that asked for a raise

ZappyFifty-four?! Yesterday this packet was forty-six. Did the cow get a promotion?

Prof. BoltPartly, yes. Get on the scooter. We'll follow your money backwards till we find out who got it.

BRRR-PUT-PUT!

The new price, from 1 Nov 2026

₹46 → ₹54a litre of Nandini toned milk (the blue packet): up ₹8, about 17%

The state Cabinet approved it on 29 October. Milk unions had asked for ₹8 to ₹10 more a litre, pointing to costlier cattle feed, fodder and vet care in a drought year.

Where the ₹8 goes

  • 🐄 ₹7 to dairy farmers. The average price paid to them goes from about ₹35 to ₹42 a litre.
  • 🏭 ₹1 to the milk unions that collect, process and pack it.

This is the fifth Nandini price rise since November 2022.

EP 2

What it costs Zappy's kitchen

The household maths

+₹480a month for a home that buys 2 litres a day (₹8 × 2 × 30 days)

Curd, buttermilk and ghee may follow, because they are made from the same milk.

MYTH"Nandini kept the whole hike for itself."

FACTUnder the government's decision, ₹7 of the ₹8 is meant to go straight on to farmers. The unions keep ₹1 for their costs.

BUSTED

ZappySo my morning coffee at the darshini will cost more?

Prof. BoltThe darshini owner is doing the same maths right now, on the back of a bill book. Be kind to him.

PART 2: FOLLOW THE RUPEEFive stops, from the booth back to the cow

EP 3

Stop 1: The milk booth, 5:30 am

Prof. BoltFirst stop: the booth. Your ₹54 lands here. The shop keeps a small cut for selling and for keeping the milk cold.

ZappyAnd the rest?

Prof. BoltGoes back up the same road the milk came down. Hold tight.

One price, everywhere

The blue packet costs the same across Karnataka, from Mysuru to Bidar. But each district milk union pays its farmers a different price, depending on its own costs and how much milk it has.

EP 4

Stop 2: The dairy, where milk gets a bath

What the dairy does with your money

  • 🚚 Tankers from village chilling centres
  • 🔥 Heating to kill germs (pasteurising)
  • ⚖️ Setting the fat to the "toned" level
  • 🛍️ Packets, ink and machines
  • 🌙 Trucks to the booths before sunrise

The Mysuru twist

9.3 lakhlitres a day collected by Mysuru's milk union (MyMUL) in April 2026

The city and district buy only about 3 lakh litres a day as packets (2024 figure). The rest goes to other cities, or is turned into milk powder and other products.

TOO MUCH MILK!

ZappyMysuru makes three times the milk it drinks?

Prof. BoltLike Mysuru Pak. We make far more than we can eat ourselves, and share the rest.

EP 5

Stop 3: The village society, 6 am sharp

Every village has a dairy cooperative society. Farmers queue up with steel cans, morning and evening. The secretary tests each can for fat and SNF (the solids other than fat), then writes down the price.

Better milk earns more per litre. Watery milk earns a frown.

How farmers get paid

  • 🏦 Straight into the bank, roughly every two weeks
  • 🏠 The union also pays the society about ₹1–2 a litre to run itself (2024 report)
  • 👩‍🌾 About 4,700 of Karnataka's societies are run by women

Zappy6 am, every single day? Even Sundays?

Prof. BoltCows don't know what a Sunday is. Nor a Deepavali.

EP 6

Stop 4: The farmer's notebook

What a farmer now gets

~₹42a litre from the union, on average (was about ₹35)
+₹5a litre more from the state government, paid separately (see Ep 9)

What it costs to make a litre

Farmers told South First in 2025 that a litre costs them about ₹30 to ₹34 to produce. Feeding one cow well costs ₹300–350 a day.

These are farmers' own estimates. Most small farmers don't keep detailed accounts.

Who are these farmers?

KMF says about 90% of its farmers are small and marginal, selling under 20 litres a day. Most own one to three cows.

78 PAISE!

So about 78 paise of every rupee you pay reaches the farmer. Amul and Mother Dairy say they pass on 75–80% of their sales to producers, so Nandini is in the same range.

ZappyThat's more than the farmer's share of my ₹300 restaurant thali.

Prof. BoltMuch more. That's the whole point of a cooperative.

ZappyAnd the other ₹12?

Prof. BoltDiesel, electricity, packets, salaries and the shop. Milk doesn't walk to Mysuru by itself.

EP 9

The ₹5 that travels separately

The Ksheeradhaare incentive

On top of the union's price, the state pays farmers an extra amount for every litre they pour.

2008: ₹2→2013: ₹4→2016: ₹5

MYTH"The ₹5 incentive is hidden inside my ₹54."

FACTIt comes from the state budget, which means from taxpayers, not from the packet. It also continues after this hike.

BUSTED

The catch

Payments have often been late. In 2024, farmer groups said about ₹800 crore was pending. Finance officials said those dues came from earlier years and would be cleared over time.

STILL WAITING...
EP 10

Nandini was born in Kodagu

The story in four steps

  • 🌄 1955: Karnataka's first dairy cooperative starts in Kudige, Kodagu.
  • 🏦 1974: The Karnataka Dairy Development Corporation is set up for a World Bank dairy project.
  • 🐄 1975: It starts in four districts: Mysuru, Hassan, Tumakuru and Bengaluru.
  • 🏷️ 1984: Renamed the Karnataka Milk Federation (KMF).

The "Anand pattern"

It copies the model made famous by Amul in Gujarat, during India's "White Revolution": three layers, each run by boards elected by farmers.

🏠 Village society→🏭 District union→🏛️ KMF

ZappySo Mysuru was there from the very first batch?

Prof. BoltOf course. We don't miss anything that involves coffee.

EP 11

How big is Nandini?

1.08 crorelitres collected in a single day: a record, on 20 May 2026
  • 🏭 16 district milk unions
  • 👩‍🌾 About 27 lakh farmer members
  • 🏠 About 16,000 village societies
  • 🥈 India's second-largest dairy cooperative, after Amul

Where the milk goes

Roughly 65 lakh litres a day leaves as milk and curd. Much of the rest becomes milk powder, ghee, butter, peda and ice cream.

HOWZAT?

At the 2024 T20 World Cup, Nandini's logo (in English and Kannada) was on the sleeves of the Scotland and Ireland teams.

ZappyScotland was playing on Mysuru milk?

Prof. BoltA bit of every Karnataka district's milk. Some people loved it. Some asked why farmers' money was sponsoring foreign teams.

EP 13

Milk at school

Back to today's special day. Under Karnataka's Ksheera Bhagya scheme, children in government and aided schools get a glass of milk, and that milk comes from Nandini.

So a dairy farmer in Mysuru district helps feed a classroom in another district.

ZappyMaulana Azad built schools and colleges. Gowri fills the glass.

Prof. BoltEducation Day and milk maths, both in one comic. Full marks, Zappy.

GULP!
EP 14

OPINION: Prof. Bolt's verdict

WHAT'S FAIR

Most of the hike goes where it should. ₹7 of ₹8 heads to farmers whose feed costs have climbed for years. A cooperative that passes on about 78 paise per rupee is doing its basic job.

WHAT STINGS

Big jumps are hard on families. ₹8 in one go hurts more than small, regular changes. And the state's ₹5 only helps farmers if it arrives on time.

MY TAKE

Pay the ₹8 without guilt: it mostly feeds Gowri. But watch two things: whether farmers' payment slips really show ₹42, and whether the ₹5 incentive stops running late.

EP 15

So what should Zappy do?

Your kitchen

  • Budget about ₹240 more a month for each daily litre.
  • Making curd at home from packet milk usually works out cheaper than buying curd.
  • Check the price on the packet, not what the shop says.

Your coffee

  • If your darshini adds ₹1–2 to a coffee, that's probably the milk, not greed.
  • Four coffees a day? Maybe three. Your heart and wallet both say thanks.

Your WhatsApp group

  • When someone says "the farmer gets nothing", share the ₹42 out of ₹54.
  • When someone says "it's all fine", share the late ₹5.

ZappyI'm sending Gowri a thank-you note.

Prof. BoltSend her some green grass instead. She can't read, but she can chew.
