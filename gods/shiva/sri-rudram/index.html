Śrī Rudram

The great hymn of the Yajur Veda, chanted at every Rudrābhiṣeka, explained line by line in three illustrated parts. Start with Part 1 and read in order.

  1. Part 1 · Namakam, anuvākas 1–5

    From Wrath to Śiva

    Bowing to Rudra's anger, the divine healer, the lord of thieves and craftsmen, and the god of pairs.

    Read ↗
  2. Part 2 · Namakam, anuvākas 6–11

    Namaḥ Śivāya and the Mahāmṛtyuñjaya

    The heart of the Rudram, the river crossing, a prayer for our families, and freedom from death.

    Read ↗
  3. Part 3 · Camakam, anuvākas 1–11

    Ca Me: And for Me

    Breath and bones, truth and joy, food and a good day, then life offered back and a vow of sweet speech.

    Read ↗
