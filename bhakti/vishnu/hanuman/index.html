Hanumān

Son of the Wind, servant of Rāma, guard at every village gate. Six parts tell his story, from the leap at the sun to Rāma’s farewell, then visit his temples; three hymns follow, verse by verse.

  1. Part 1 of 6

    Who Hanumān Is

    His names, whose son he is, his forms and the five-faced Pañcamukha, the cirañjīvī belief, the Mahābhārata, Jain and Thai tellings, worship and Jayantī dates.

    Read ↗
  2. Part 2 of 6

    Birth and Boyhood

    Añjanā and the Wind, the leap at the sun, Indra’s thunderbolt and the broken jaw, the gods’ boons, the curse of forgetting, and four birthplace claims.

    Read ↗
  3. Part 3 of 6

    Kiṣkindhā

    Meeting Rāma in disguise, “not one wrong word”, the fire-vow with Sugrīva, Vālī’s death, the rains, the ring, Sampāti and Jāmbavān’s reminder.

    Read ↗
  4. Part 4 of 6

    The Leap to Laṅkā

    The Sundara Kāṇḍa scene by scene: Surasā, Laṅkinī, the night search, Sītā found, the ring and crest-jewel, Laṅkā on fire, “Seen Sītā”.

    Read ↗
  5. Part 5 of 6

    The War and After

    Vibhīṣaṇa’s refuge, the bridge, two mountains of herbs, Ahirāvaṇa, news to Sītā, the pearl necklace, Rāma in his heart, Rāma’s farewell.

    Read ↗
  6. Part 6 of 6

    Hanumān’s Temples

    Saṅkaṭ Mocan, Hanumāngaṛhī, Sālāsar, Jakhu, Añjanādri and Yantroddhāraka at Hampi, Gāli Āñjaneya, Nāmakkal, Suchindram and more.

    Saṅkaṭmocan Aṣṭak
