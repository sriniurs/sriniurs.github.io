VARĀHA

DAŚĀVATĀRA · A REFERENCE GUIDE
The boar who lifted the Earth

FOREWORD

The third avatāra dives. The demon Hiraṇyākṣa had dragged the Earth down into the bottom of the cosmic ocean. To bring her back, Viṣṇu came out of Brahmā’s nostril as a boar no bigger than a thumb, and in a moment grew as large as a mountain.

Varāha plunged into the waters, fought the demon for a thousand years, and rose with the Earth goddess Bhūdevī resting on his tusk. That image, the boar-headed god lifting a small, grateful Earth, is one of the grandest in Indian art, carved in stone at Udayagiri in 401 CE. Kings of Karnataka took the boar as their emblem, and the gold coins of Vijayanagara were called varāhas.

This guide tells the story and follows the boar through art, history and temples.

Keśava dhṛta-śūkara-rūpa,
srini.presents 🙏

ZAPPY: A pig saves the world?

PROF. BOLT: A wild boar, digging where no one else could. The humblest animal does the greatest rescue.

THE CURSED GATEKEEPERS

Jaya and Vijaya
WhoJaya and Vijaya, the gatekeepers of Viṣṇu’s heaven, Vaikuṇṭha
The insultThey blocked the four child-sages, the Kumāras, from seeing Viṣṇu
The curseTo be born on earth, away from the Lord
The choiceSeven births as devotees, or three as his enemies; they chose three, to return sooner
Three birthsHiraṇyākṣa and Hiraṇyakaśipu; Rāvaṇa and Kumbhakarṇa; Śiśupāla and Dantavakra

ZAPPY: They chose to be his enemies?

PROF. BOLT: Three lives of hating him was the faster way home. Even enmity keeps your mind on God.

The brother: Narasiṃha and Hiraṇyakaśipu

THE SUNKEN EARTH

Hiraṇyākṣa
WhoHiraṇyākṣa, “golden-eyed”, the first-born of the twin demons of Diti
BoonMade mighty by Brahmā’s boon, he roamed the worlds looking for a fight
The crimeHe seized the Earth and dragged her into the depths of the cosmic waters
Another viewIn other tellings the Earth simply lay under the waters of dissolution, and Brahmā needed it raised to begin creation

THE BOAR FROM BRAHMĀ’S NOSE

A tiny boar becomes a giant
The sneezeAs Brahmā and the sages worried, a boar the size of a thumb came out of Brahmā’s nostril
The growthIn a moment it grew as big as an elephant, then as big as a mountain
The roarIts grunt filled the worlds, and the sages praised it with the Vedas
The diveSniffing for the Earth like a boar for a root, it plunged into the ocean

ZAPPY: God came out of a sneeze?

PROF. BOLT: The Bhāgavata loves surprises. Small beginnings, cosmic results, again.

THE RESCUE

The Earth on his tusk
The finding. At the bottom of the ocean, Varāha found the Earth and lifted her on his tusks.
The challenge. Hiraṇyākṣa attacked, mocking him as a beast of the swamp. Varāha first set the Earth safely on the water.
The fight. They fought with mace and discus, in a battle the Bhāgavata says lasted a thousand years.
The end. Varāha struck the demon down with his hand. He set the Earth back in place, and Brahmā could begin creation again.

BHŪDEVĪ

The Earth as his consort
BhūdevīThe Earth goddess, one of Viṣṇu’s two consorts with Śrīdevī (Lakṣmī)
In artShe sits on Varāha’s arm or shoulder, small and grateful, gazing at him
Varāha PurāṇaTold by Varāha to Bhūdevī as she rested on his tusk
Their sonIn the Purāṇas, Naraka was born to Varāha and Bhūdevī; his defeat by Kṛṣṇa is remembered on Naraka Caturdaśī, part of Dīpāvalī
ĀṇḍāḷŚrī Vaiṣṇavas see the poet-saint Āṇḍāḷ as Bhūdevī born on earth

THE VEDIC BOAR

Emūṣa
Taittirīya SaṃhitāPrajāpati becomes a boar and raises the earth from the waters
Śatapatha BrāhmaṇaA boar named Emūṣa raised the earth, which was then only the size of a hand
ṚgvedaA boar is mentioned in hymns to Indra and Viṣṇu
Yajña VarāhaLater texts make the boar’s body the sacrifice itself: his tusks the sacrificial posts, his tongue the fire

UDAYAGIRI, 401 CE

The great boar in stone
WhereCave 5, Udayagiri caves near Vidiśā, Madhya Pradesh
WhenGupta period; an inscription in Cave 6 dates the site’s consecration to 401 CE under Chandragupta II
WhatA colossal man-boar lifting Bhūdevī, with rows of gods and sages watching and the rivers Gaṅgā and Yamunā flowing to the ocean
MeaningScholars read it also as royal art: the Gupta king as the rescuer of the earth
EranAt nearby Eran stands a huge free-standing boar statue of about 500 CE, covered with small figures of sages

THE BOAR OF KARNATAKA

Emblem of kings
BādāmiThe Chalukyas of Bādāmi took the boar (varāha lāñchana) as their royal emblem; Bādāmi Cave 2 and 3 (6th c.) carve Varāha in stone
VijayanagaraThe boar, with sun, moon and sword, was Vijayanagara’s emblem; its gold coins were called varāhas (the “pagodas” of European traders)
MysoreVarāha coins and the boar remained known in the Mysore region for centuries
MeaningA king lifts and protects the land, as Varāha lifted the Earth

ZAPPY: So money in Karnataka was once named after Varāha?

PROF. BOLT: For centuries. A varāha was a gold coin.

MORE GREAT VARĀHAS

Mahābalipuram and Khajuraho
MahābalipuramThe Varāha cave, a 7th-century Pallava rock-cut shrine with a fine Bhū Varāha panel
KhajurahoA small 10th-century shrine holds a great free-standing boar, its body carved with hundreds of tiny gods
ElloraVarāha panels in the Daśāvatāra cave
Two stylesTheriomorphic (a whole boar) in the north; man-boar in most of India

VARĀHA TEMPLES

Where he is worshipped
ŚrīmuṣṇamBhū Varāha Svāmī temple, Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu: a self-manifested (svayam vyakta) image
TirumalaTradition says Śrīnivāsa received his place on the hill from Varāha, and promised that pilgrims would see Varāha first
KallahalliThe Bhū Varāhanātha temple near Kṛṣṇarājapet, Maṇḍya district, Karnataka, with a large image of Varāha and Bhūdevī
OthersVarāha shrines at Puṣkar and Khajuraho; Vārāhī, his Śakti, is one of the Mātṛkās

WHAT VARĀHA TEACHES

The wisdom of the boar
Dig deepHe went to the bottom to find what was lost
Care for the EarthThe earth is a goddess to be lifted, not dragged down
No form is lowGod chose the body of a wild boar
Protect first, fight secondHe set the Earth safe before facing the demon

ZAPPY: What should I take from Varāha?

PROF. BOLT: When the world sinks, go down after it. And put the Earth somewhere safe before you fight.
