Pañca Kedār

The Pāṇḍavas chased Śiva; he dived into the Himalaya as a bull. His hump, arms, face, navel and hair rose in five valleys. Start with the overview, then visit each Kedār.

    Śiva’s Five Himalayan Shrines

    Kedārnāth

    The hump-shaped rock liṅga, Jyotirliṅga and Char Dhām, the 2013 flood and Bhīm Śilā, the Karnataka Rāval.

  3. Kedār 2 of 5 · the arms

    Tuṅganāth

    The highest Śiva temple, below Candraśilā where Rāma meditated; local Makkumath priests.

  4. Kedār 3 of 5 · the face

    Rudranāth

    Nīlakaṇṭha’s face in a rock cave among high meadows; the Vaitaraṇī and offerings for ancestors.

  5. Kedār 4 of 5 · the navel

    Madhyamaheśvar

    The navel-shaped liṅga below Chaukhamba, Būḍhā Madhyamaheśvar and its holy water.

  6. Kedār 5 of 5 · the hair

    Kalpeśvar

    Śiva’s jaṭā in a cave in the Urgam valley, the wish-tree, and the only Kedār open all year.
