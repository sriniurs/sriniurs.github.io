BHUVANEŚVARĪ

DAŚA MAHĀVIDYĀ · A REFERENCE GUIDE
Queen of the worlds, Mother of Kannada

FOREWORD

Kālī is time. The fourth Great Wisdom is space: Bhuvaneśvarī, Queen of the three worlds, the golden, smiling Mother in whom everything has room to be. In the Devī Bhāgavata, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva flew to her jewel island and came back knowing whose power they wielded.

In Karnataka she is something more: Kannada Tāyi, Mother Kannada. Her image stands at Hampi; a Mahārāja built her a temple at the gate of the Mysuru Palace; every first of November the whole state honours her.

This guide tells her stories, her meaning and her temples.

Jai Bhuvaneśvarī,
srini.presents 🙏

ZAPPY: The Goddess honoured on Kannada Rājyotsava is a Mahāvidyā?

PROF. BOLT: The very same Bhuvaneśvarī. Few people know the Tantric Queen of the Worlds is also Kannada Tāyi.

Part of Daśa Mahāvidyā, the ten Great Wisdoms. Previous: Tripurasundarī.

ZAPPY: A Goddess who is empty space?

PROF. BOLT: Not empty: spacious. Every star, every thought needs room. She is the room.

HER NAMES

Queen of the three worlds
BhuvanaThe worlds: tribhuvana, the three of earth (bhūḥ), sky (bhuvaḥ) and heaven (svaḥ)
ĪśvarīSovereign, queen
Hrīṃkārī“She who is the sound hrīṃ”: a name in the Lalitā Sahasranāma
Ādi Parāśakti“Supreme primal power”: her title in the Devī Bhāgavata
Kannada Tāyi · Nāḍa Devī“Mother Kannada”, “Goddess of the land”: her Karnataka names
BhuvaneśvaraHer consort: Śiva as Lord of the worlds

ZAPPY: So bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ in the Gāyatrī is her?

PROF. BOLT: Those are her three worlds. She is the queen of exactly what the Gāyatrī names.

HER FORM

The gentle sovereign
ColourGolden, or red like the rising sun

Noose and goadShe draws beings in, and guides them

THE GODDESS OF SPACE

Ākāśa, the first element
ĀkāśaOf the five elements, space (ākāśa) comes first; the others arise within it
Kālī and BhuvaneśvarīKālī is time; Bhuvaneśvarī is space: together, the frame of the world
Room to beNothing can exist without space, so she is the ground of every created thing
The heartTeachers say her true seat is the space of the heart (hṛdayākāśa)
Fourth wisdomAfter Kālī, Tārā and Tripurasundarī, the Goddess as the world’s own vastness

ZAPPY: Time first, then space?

PROF. BOLT: Kālī ends things; Bhuvaneśvarī makes room for them. Between them, a universe.

MAṆIDVĪPA AND THE TRIMŪRTI

The gods who became women
A question. In the Devī Bhāgavata, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva once wondered who was greatest. A celestial chariot carried them across the ocean of nectar to Maṇidvīpa.
The vision. There they saw the Goddess, Bhuvaneśvarī, enthroned. Under her gaze the three gods found themselves turned into women, attendants of the Goddess.
The gift. Humbled, they praised her. She gave each his power: Sarasvatī to Brahmā, Lakṣmī to Viṣṇu, Kālī (Gaurī) to Śiva, and sent them to create, keep and end the worlds.

ZAPPY: The Trimūrti work for her?

PROF. BOLT: In the Devī Bhāgavata, yes. Every creator, keeper and destroyer is borrowing her power.

THE DEVĪ GĪTĀ

The Goddess teaches
WhereBook 7 of the Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa
SettingThe Goddess appears to Himavat, king of the mountains, in her cosmic form as Bhuvaneśvarī
TeachingShe explains that she is Brahman, the self of all; she teaches yoga, devotion and knowledge
ModelBuilt on the Bhagavad Gītā, with the Goddess in Kṛṣṇa’s place
Cosmic formLike Kṛṣṇa’s viśvarūpa, she shows the worlds within her body

HER BOND WITH TRIPURASUNDARĪ

The Sun’s helper
A modern storyA Hindi source retold by Kinsley: the sages gave Soma to the Sun; empowered by Tripurasundarī, the Sun made the three worlds
ThenThe Goddess “took an appropriate form and pervaded and directed the triple world”: that form is Bhuvaneśvarī
SoBhuvaneśvarī is Tripurasundarī as she governs the created world
In ŚrīvidyāThe two are worshipped side by side; hrīṃ belongs to both
Previous: Tripurasundarī

KANNADA TĀYI

The Goddess of Karnataka
Mother KannadaBhuvaneśvarī is revered as Kannada Tāyi or Nāḍa Devī, the deity of Kannada identity and of the state
VijayanagaraBy tradition she blessed Harihara and Bukka in founding the empire; an image of her stands in the Virūpākṣa temple, Hampi
RājyotsavaOn 1 November, Karnataka’s formation day, her image is honoured across the state, including at the Vidhāna Saudha
Official imageThe state government has adopted an official image of Bhuvaneśvarī as Karnāṭaka Māte

ZAPPY: A Mahāvidyā is Karnataka's mother?

PROF. BOLT: The Queen of the Worlds became the queen of one land too. Kannadigas call her Tāyi.

MYSURU AND BHUVANAGIRI

Her Karnataka temples
Mysuru PalaceA Bhuvaneśvarī temple at the north gate of the palace fort, built in 1951 by Mahārāja Jayacāmarājendra Woḍeyar, matching the Varāhasvāmi temple on the south side
Sūrya MaṇḍalaA copper Sun-disc moved from the palace into the temple by the Mahārāja
RājyotsavaOn 1 November she is dressed in finery; in 2020 she received a new silver kavaca
BhuvanagiriA 17th-century hilltop temple near Siddāpur, Uttara Kannaḍa: said to be the only one in Karnataka with daily worship of Bhuvaneśvarī, and the centre of Rājyotsava rites
HampiThe image in the Virūpākṣa complex is honoured but has no regular daily rites
More Mysuru: Temples around Mysuru
