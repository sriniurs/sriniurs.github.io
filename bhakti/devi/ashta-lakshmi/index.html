Aṣṭa Lakṣmī

One Goddess of fortune, eight gifts: grain, courage, royal grace, family, victory, knowledge, money, and the first Lakṣmī behind them all. Start with the overview, then meet each form.

    The Eight Forms of Lakṣmī

    Who Lakṣmī is, the Śrī Sūkta and the churning, why eight and how the lists differ, her days and the Aṣṭalakṣmī temples.

  2. Form 1 of 8

    Ādi Lakṣmī

    The primordial Lakṣmī: Bhṛgu’s daughter, Viṣṇu’s eternal consort, the merciful mediator, Padmāvatī of Tirumala.

    Read ↗
  3. Form 2 of 8

    Dhānya Lakṣmī

    Grain and harvest: the Śrī Sūkta’s fertile Śrī, Annapūrṇā, Saṅkrānti and Pongal, never wasting food.

    Read ↗
  4. Form 3 of 8

    Dhairya Lakṣmī

    Courage, also Vīra Lakṣmī: the eight-armed warrior, close to Durgā; Sītā, Sāvitrī and Prahlāda.

    Read ↗
  5. Form 4 of 8

    Gaja Lakṣmī

    Bathed by elephants: the oldest image of Lakṣmī, from Bharhut and Sāñcī to coins and Hoysaḷa door lintels.

    Read ↗
  6. Form 5 of 8

    Santāna Lakṣmī

    Children and family: Kardama in the Śrī Sūkta, Varalakṣmī Vratam and Cārumatī, Santāna Gopāla.

    Read ↗
  7. Form 6 of 8

    Vijaya Lakṣmī

    Victory: Vijayadaśamī, Mysuru Dasara, Arjuna “Vijaya”, Rāja Lakṣmī and the city of Vijayanagara.

    Read ↗
  8. Form 7 of 8

    Vidyā Lakṣmī

    Knowledge: not Sarasvatī but learning as fortune; Lakṣmī Hayagrīva, vidyārambha and the wealth no thief can take.

    Read ↗
  9. Form 8 of 8

    Dhana Lakṣmī

    Money and gold: artha, Kubera’s loan, Śaṅkara’s rain of gold, Dhanteras and Dīpāvalī; earn rightly, give freely.
