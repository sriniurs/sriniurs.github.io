What One Laddoo Does to Your Body in 2 Hours

Zappy has a full box of Dhanteras sweets and one question: what actually happens inside me after the first bite? Prof. Bolt starts a stopwatch.

The Human MachineDiwali SweetsBlood SugarHow It Works
TODAY IS SPECIAL!It's Dhanteras and Dhanvantari Jayanti: happy birthday to the god of medicine, who rose from the ocean carrying a pot of amrit 🪔 Today we look at what's in your pot of sweets.

PART 1: THE LADDOO CLOCKMinute by minute, inside your body

EP 1

Meet the laddoo

ZappyIt's just one small laddoo. How much damage can a ball of besan do?

Prof. BoltNo damage, if you know the rules. First, let's see what you're holding.

Inside one besan laddoo

🫘 Besan (gram flour)+🧈 Ghee+🍚 Sugar

A shop laddoo weighs about 40–50 g and carries roughly 170–210 calories, about a small meal.

Depending on size and recipe, that's about 10–20 g of sugar.

WAIT, REALLY?

India's nutrition institute (ICMR-NIN) says an adult should have only about 20–25 g of added sugar a whole day.

One big laddoo can use up most of it.

Calories: nutritionist estimates in Business Standard (Oct 2025). Sugar range: our estimate from published recipe data.

EP 2

Minute 0: your brain says "yes!" before you swallow

The reward rush

The moment sugar touches your tongue, your brain releases dopamine, the chemical of reward and "do that again".

Scientists at Germany's Max Planck Institute found this happens in two waves: one as you taste, before the food even reaches your stomach, and another once it gets there.

PLOT TWIST

Ever said "I'm full, but there's always room for a sweet"? Another Max Planck study, in the journal Science, found the brain cells that make you feel full also release a feel-good chemical when they sense sugar.

So the "dessert stomach" is real. It's in your head, not your belly.

ZappySo my brain is a laddoo fan before my stomach has a say?

Prof. BoltFor millions of years, sugar was rare and gave quick energy. Your brain learned to grab it. It hasn't heard about sweet shops.

EP 3

Minutes 15–90: the sugar rises

How it works

Your gut splits table sugar into two smaller sugars: glucose (the body's fuel) and fructose (which mostly goes to the liver).

Glucose slips into your blood. Your blood sugar starts rising within about 15–30 minutes and usually peaks around 60–90 minutes.

The laddoo clock

0 min: taste, dopamine→15–30 min: sugar enters blood→60–90 min: the peak→2–3 hrs: back to normal*

*In a healthy person. Timings vary with the food, the person and what else is in the stomach.

GOOD NEWS

The ghee and besan help a little. Fat and protein slow down how fast your stomach empties, so a laddoo usually hits the blood more gently than sugar in a cold drink.

More gently, not harmlessly. The sugar still arrives.

EP 4

Insulin: the key that unlocks your cells

The pancreas sends in the key

Your pancreas, a small organ behind the stomach, senses the rising sugar and releases insulin.

Think of insulin as a key. It unlocks your muscle and liver cells so glucose can move out of the blood and into them.

Where the sugar goes

⚡ Used now, for energy→📦 Stored in liver and muscles→🍩 Extra turned into fat

Liver and muscle stores are like a cupboard: they fill up. Whatever doesn't fit, the liver turns into fat.

ZappySo one laddoo becomes energy, and five laddoos become... me, but rounder?

Prof. BoltRoughly, yes. The body is a good accountant. Nothing goes to waste.

EP 5

Hours 2–4: the crash and the craving

When the key works too well

A big, fast sugar rush can bring a big flood of insulin. Sometimes it clears the sugar so well that your blood sugar dips below where it started.

Doctors call this reactive hypoglycaemia: a sugar dip that usually comes 2 to 4 hours after a carb-heavy snack.

YAWN...

You may feel tired, shaky, foggy or grumpy. And very hungry for... another sweet.

Sweet→Spike→Crash→Craving→Sweet

ZappyThat's why I'm asleep on the sofa by 4 pm every Diwali!

Prof. BoltPartly. Not everyone crashes, and the big lunch helps too. But the sweet-crash-sweet loop is how one laddoo becomes six.

EP 6

When the key gets stuck

Two ways it goes wrong

In type 2 diabetes, the cells stop answering insulin's knock properly, and the pancreas can't keep up. So the sugar from that laddoo stays in the blood longer and higher.

Prediabetes is the warning stage: sugar is above normal, but not yet diabetes.

India's numbers (ICMR-INDIAB, 2023)

101 MIndians with diabetes
136 Mwith prediabetes

That's about 1 in 9 adults with diabetes and 1 in 7 with prediabetes. Karnataka is one of the southern states above 10%.

HOPE

ICMR's chief said prediabetes is completely reversible, and about 60% of type 2 diabetes can be reversed with diet and activity.

Most people with prediabetes don't know they have it. A simple blood test tells you.

PART 2: WHAT ELSE IS IN THE BOX?Sugar isn't the only thing to check

EP 7

The khoya trap

Why festive sweets get faked

Diwali demand shoots up, and so does cheating. Food-safety guides warn that khoya (and chhena) can be bulked up with starch, fine flour, refined oil or skimmed milk powder.

On 29 Sept 2026, the food regulator FSSAI ordered a special festive drive across India, marking it "most important".

Karnataka's drive this October

286sweet units checked
383samples to labs

40 notices issued and 30 kg of wrongly labelled khova seized, in just three days (15–17 Oct 2026).

TRY THIS AT HOME

FSSAI's home test: boil a little khoya with water, let it cool, add 2–3 drops of tincture of iodine.

Turns blue? Starch has been added.

From FSSAI's DART booklet of home tests.

EP 8

Colours, silver and the date on the tray

Bright colours

In 2024 Karnataka tested 171 gobi manchurian samples. 107 had unsafe artificial colours. Of 25 cotton candy samples, 15 failed, some with Rhodamine B, an industrial dye.

Karnataka banned these colours, with up to 7 years in jail and a ₹10 lakh fine. Very bright sweets deserve a second look.

The silver leaf (varq)

Since 2016, FSSAI rules say edible silver leaf must be 99.9% pure silver, very thin, and made without any animal parts. It was once hammered between sheets of animal gut.

A 2005 Lucknow study found only 46% of 178 samples were pure enough.

The tray rule

FSSAI says loose sweets must show a "best before" date on the tray. No date? Ask for one.

ZappyI never once looked at the tray!

Prof. BoltStart this Dhanteras. It takes 3 seconds.

EP 10

India invented the sweet tooth

Sugar's Indian passport

Around 350 CE, in the Gupta era, Indians learned to boil cane juice into dry crystals that could be stored and traded. Earlier texts suggest refined sugar existed even before that.

The crystals were called khanda, which gave English the word candy. The Sanskrit sharkara became sugar.

WAIT, REALLY?

In 647 CE, China sent missions to India to learn how to refine sugar. A Greek doctor in the 1st century described it as a kind of hardened honey from Indian reeds.

Ancient Ayurveda even named diabetes madhumeha, "honey urine".

Then and now

Then: a luxury, traded like a spice. Now: India is the world's largest sugar consumer, about 28 million tonnes a year, and Karnataka is the 3rd-biggest producer after Maharashtra and UP.

EAT THIS, THEN THAT

The 2015 study's lead doctor put it neatly: instead of telling people "don't eat that", say "eat this before that".

Why walking works

Working muscles pull glucose out of the blood for fuel. Walking doesn't erase the laddoo, but it flattens the spike.

EP 12

What the experts say: how much is too much?

The official limits

  • ICMR-NIN (India, 2024): added sugar under 5% of daily calories, about 20–25 g a day.
  • WHO: under 10%, ideally under 5%.
  • NIN and FSSAI posters now say 25 g ≈ 5 teaspoons. The old limit was 50 g; it was halved because of rising lifestyle diseases.

India's sugar per person, per day

India's sugar
~55 g
ICMR limit
25 g

India uses about 20 kg of sugar per person per year (ISMA). 20 kg ÷ 365 ≈ 55 g a day: our arithmetic. Not all of it is eaten (some goes into industry, some is wasted), but it's more than double the limit on paper.

TURNING POINT?

India's sugar use has stopped growing as fast. ISMA expected consumption to rise only about 1.4% in 2025-26, against about 4% a year before Covid, partly because people are more health-aware.

About 60–65% of India's sugar now goes into biscuits, drinks, sweets and other packaged foods, not the home sugar tin.

EP 13

The Karnataka angle

We grow it

Karnataka is India's 3rd-largest sugar producer, about 4.8 million tonnes in 2025-26 (ISMA estimate), from Belagavi, Bagalkot, Mandya and other cane belts.

We feel it

Diabetes is above 10% of adults in Karnataka (ICMR-INDIAB). Southern states have some of India's highest rates.

And our own Mysore Pak and Dharwad peda are on every Deepavali plate.

We're checking it

Karnataka's food-safety teams have run repeated sweet-shop drives this year. In one drive on 31 Aug 2026, six sweet units were shut temporarily for expired stock, poor labels, flies and unauthorised colours.

The October drive is in EP 7.

EP 14

OPINION: Prof. Bolt's verdict

EAT IT

One laddoo on Dhanteras won't hurt a healthy person. Your body handles it in about 2–3 hours. Enjoy it without guilt. Festivals are for sweets.

THE REAL DANGER

It's not the first laddoo. It's the fifth, and the box that lasts two weeks. The spike-crash-craving loop does the damage, plus fake khoya and dodgy colours.

MY TAKE

Mind the clock, not just the calories. Eat after a meal, eat it slowly, then walk. Same sweet, gentler spike.

EP 15

So what should Zappy do?

Before the bite

  • Eat sweets after a meal, not on an empty stomach.
  • Veggies and dal first, sweet last.
  • Cut it in half. Share the other half.
  • Jaggery and honey sweets are still sugar.

After the bite

  • Walk for 10 minutes, even just around the house.
  • Wait 20 minutes before reaching for the next one. The craving often passes.
  • Have diabetes? Ask your doctor about festive sweets. Don't skip your medicines.

At the shop

  • Look for the FSSAI licence number.
  • Check the best-before date on the tray.
  • Be wary of very bright colours.
  • Iodine test on khoya: blue means starch.

ZappySo I'm allowed the laddoo, I just have to walk it home?

Prof. BoltExactly. Happy Dhanteras.
