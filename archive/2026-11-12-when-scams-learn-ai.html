ZAPPYWere all banks hacked?

Technical attacks on organisations differ from AI-generated impersonation of customers.

THE DEEP DIVEFive research-rich questions
EP 7

Deep Dive: the actors

ZAPPYCan a tool identify the attacker?
PROF. BOLTTool use alone is not reliable attribution.

Reuters reported the name Crafty Falcon; CERT-In did not attribute the group to a country or state.

EP 8

Deep Dive: the tools

ZAPPYDo these names mean my phone is infected?
PROF. BOLTNo. They describe a reported campaign, not your device.

The report named ARTEX, PentAGI, Cairn and CyberStrikeAI as tools in the alert.

EP 9

Deep Dive: the timing

ZAPPYAre holiday messages fake?
PROF. BOLTNot automatically. Verify unexpected requests.

The report warned that attackers may exploit weekends and holidays when response is slower.

EP 10

Deep Dive: voice cloning

ZAPPYHow do I check?
PROF. BOLTCall the person on a number already saved.

Synthetic speech can imitate a person's voice; emotion and noisy calls can make judgement harder.

EP 11

Deep Dive: payment security

ZAPPYWhat must I never share?
PROF. BOLTOTP, PIN, password or screen-sharing access.

A name, photograph or convincing voice does not authorise access to your account.

PART 3 — YOUR MOVEOpinion, action, the final twist
EP 12

OPINION: Bolt's verdict

ZAPPYWhat's your verdict?
PROF. BOLTMy opinion: pause for two minutes before sending money.

GOOD NEWS: simple independent callbacks are powerful. WATCH OUT: authentic-sounding media can mislead.

TAKEAWAY: A convincing voice is not identity proof.
EP 13

So what should Zappy do?

ZAPPYWhat if money is gone?
PROF. BOLTContact the bank immediately; report at 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

End suspicious calls. Open your bank app yourself. Never follow an unsolicited payment link.

WATCH OUT: Never disclose OTPs or PINs.
EP 14

The family drill

ZAPPYIs a secret phrase enough?
PROF. BOLTHelpful, but not foolproof. Confirm independently.

Agree on a callback routine and rehearse it with older relatives.

EP 15

The smartest button

ZAPPYWhat's my rule?
PROF. BOLTStop. Check. Then act.

Fraud thrives on panic. The simplest defence is slowing down.
