Reverse the telescope

ZAPPYAnd today's crore in 2000 money?

PART 2 · THE MATHSWhat the number can actually pay
EP 5

The doubling trick

ZAPPYFour percent inflation sounds tiny.
PROF. BOLTAt a constant 4% for 20 years, prices multiply by roughly 2.19. ₹50,000 monthly expenses become about ₹1.10 lakh.
BOOM! Compounding works on prices, too.
EP 6

A crore is not a monthly salary

ZAPPYCan I withdraw ₹1 lakh every month?
PROF. BOLTThat is ₹12 lakh a year, or 12% of the starting corpus. It can be an aggressive draw on retirement savings.
WATCH OUT: A 12% withdrawal rate is not a safe-return promise.
EP 7

A smaller withdrawal

ZAPPYWhat about ₹25,000 monthly?
PROF. BOLTThat's ₹3 lakh a year, or 3% initially. It is less demanding, but no withdrawal percentage guarantees success.
EP 8

Two families, one crore

ZAPPYWould Mysuru and Mumbai families feel equally rich?
PROF. BOLTNo. Rent, debt, medical care, dependants and lifestyle make the same corpus feel very different.
THE DEEP DIVEFive questions behind the headline
EP 9

DEEP DIVE: Whose inflation?

ZAPPYIs everyone's inflation identical?
PROF. BOLTNo. Headline CPI is an average basket. Your personal spending on health, education and housing may rise differently.
EP 10

DEEP DIVE: The RBI target

ZAPPYDoes India promise only 4% inflation?
PROF. BOLTNo. The government retained a 4% CPI inflation target with a 2–6% tolerance band for April 2026–March 2031. It is a policy objective, not a guarantee.
EP 11

DEEP DIVE: Twenty years later

ZAPPYHow much is ₹1 crore worth after 20 years?
PROF. BOLTAt a hypothetical steady 4% inflation, its purchasing power is like ₹45.6 lakh today. At 6%, like ₹31.2 lakh. These are scenarios, not predictions.
EP 12

DEEP DIVE: Real returns

ZAPPYIf an investment earns 8% and inflation is 4%, is my real return 4%?
PROF. BOLTThe exact pre-tax real return is 1.08 divided by 1.04, minus one: about 3.85%. Returns can fluctuate and losses are possible.
EP 13

DEEP DIVE: The market trap

ZAPPYWhy not put everything in the highest-return fund?
PROF. BOLTBecause a fall early in retirement while withdrawing money can be damaging. Diversification and a liquid spending reserve can help manage risk.
PART 3 · THE VERDICTThe practical way forward
EP 14

OPINION: Prof. Bolt's verdict

ZAPPYSo ₹1 crore is not wealth anymore?
PROF. BOLTIt absolutely is meaningful wealth. But being 'rich' means having enough for your actual life, with a margin for uncertainty—not hitting a round number.
GOOD NEWS: A crore is a strong start. MY TAKE: Match your plan to expenses and time.
EP 15

So what should Zappy do?

ZAPPYGive me the practical checklist!
PROF. BOLTCalculate annual spending, account for inflation and healthcare, check debt and emergency cash, and stress-test retirement withdrawals. Revisit every year.
