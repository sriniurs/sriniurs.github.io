The new twist

ZAPPY · LEFTHave fees started today?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTA proposed 0.4% merchant discount rate on certain merchant payments above ₹2,000 was postponed until after Deepavali. A separate start-date notification was promised.
EP 3

The chai-stall test

ZAPPY · LEFTWill my ₹20 tea become ₹20.08?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTNo. Payments of ₹2,000 or less remain outside the proposed fee, as do person-to-person transfers.
EP 4

Who pays?

ZAPPY · LEFTWhat is MDR?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTMerchant Discount Rate is a fee borne by eligible businesses for payment acceptance—not a tax automatically added to every customer's UPI transfer.

EP 5

Do the maths

ZAPPY · LEFTWhat about ₹5,000?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTAt the proposed 0.4%, that means ₹20 in merchant fees. It is not automatically ₹20 added to the buyer's bill.
₹5,000 × 0.4% = ₹20 (illustrative merchant fee)
EP 6

The ₹2,000 line

ZAPPY · LEFTCan I split a big bill?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTDon't rely on that. Final operating rules, merchant classification and payment purpose matter. Pay legitimate invoices normally.
EP 7

Three lanes

ZAPPY · LEFTIs all UPI the same?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTThink of a transfer to a friend, a small purchase, and a larger eligible shop purchase. The proposal targets the third lane.
EP 8

A record month

ZAPPY · LEFTWhy this fuss now?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTIn October 2026, NPCI recorded about 2,547 crore UPI transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore. The network is enormous.
THE DEEP DIVE
EP 9

DEEP DIVE: Growth

ZAPPY · LEFTIs that a record?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTYes. October's volume was around 23% higher than October 2025. But total UPI volume includes payments that would never face the proposed fee.
EP 10

DEEP DIVE: Small sellers

ZAPPY · LEFTWhat about street vendors?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTThe framework includes protections for small payments and certain small merchants. A QR sticker alone cannot tell you which category applies.
EP 11

DEEP DIVE: Why fees?

ZAPPY · LEFTWhy charge at all?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTPayment infrastructure, fraud prevention and uptime cost money. But merchant groups fear extra costs could discourage digital payments.
EP 12

DEEP DIVE: Date trap

ZAPPY · LEFTDoes 'after Diwali' mean today?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTNo. That phrase is a window, not an effective-date notice. The 13 October postponement announcement promised a separately notified date.
WATCH OUT: Do not mistake an announced plan for a confirmed start date.
EP 13

DEEP DIVE: Hidden costs

ZAPPY · LEFTCan a shop add a fee?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTAsk for an itemised bill. A merchant's price choices, payment-network rules and customer protections are different questions.
PART 3 · THE VERDICT
EP 14

OPINION: Prof. Bolt's verdict

ZAPPY · LEFTGood or bad?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTGOOD NEWS: everyday payments remain protected. WATCH OUT: merchants may face costs and rumours. MY TAKE: publish clear rules and guard against hidden surcharges.
OPINION: Protect small payments, clarify costs and prevent hidden charges.
EP 15

Your move

ZAPPY · LEFTWhat should I do?
PROF. BOLT · RIGHTKeep using UPI normally. Verify recipients, check amounts, request a bill, and look for dated official notices before forwarding fee rumours.
