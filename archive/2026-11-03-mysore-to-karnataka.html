Why Isn't It Still Mysore? The 17-Year Name Fight

Karnataka just turned 71. But on its first birthday, in 1956, it was called Mysore, and it stayed Mysore for 17 years. A curious young learner from Mysuru wants to know why the name changed. A wise mentor who remembers the old maps explains.

THE PASTFrom 20 pieces to one state

EP 1

A language cut into pieces

ZappyMy grandfather's old school book says "Mysore State". Did Karnataka not exist back then?

Prof. BoltThe land existed. The people spoke Kannada. But under British rule, they lived under many different rulers.

Before 1947

20 pieces

Deccan Herald counts Kannada speakers spread over about 20 fragments: the Nizam's Hyderabad, the Mysore kingdom, the Bombay and Madras presidencies, Coorg and several small princely states.

The dream begins

  • 1890: R.H. Deshpande starts the Karnataka Vidyavardhaka Sangha in Dharwad, to keep Kannada alive in a Marathi-run area.
  • 1905: Alur Venkata Rao starts the "Ekikarana" (unification) movement.
  • 1924: Congress meets in Belagavi, and the Karnataka Pradesh Congress Committee is formed.
EP 2

Why "Mysore" was a proud name

The Mysore model (under Krishna Raja Wadiyar IV)

1902 Shivanasamudra power plant→1905 Bengaluru's first electric streetlight→1911 IISc starts work→1913 Bank of Mysore→1916 Mysore University→1924 KRS dam
WOW!

At the 1930 Round Table Conference, Lord Sankey called Mysore "the best administered state in the world".

Prof. BoltSo when the time came to join the Kannada lands, many people in old Mysore did not want to lose that famous name.

EP 3

1952–1956: India redraws its map by language

The spark

Potti Sriramulu died on 16 December 1952 after a fast for a Telugu state. Andhra State came in 1953, and Bellary district moved from Madras to Mysore.

The commission

The States Reorganisation Commission (Fazal Ali, K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru) was set up in December 1953 and reported on 30 September 1955.

The result

14 states+6 UTs

The States Reorganisation Act, 1956 redrew India. "UT" means union territory, run by the Centre.

Bigger state, same old name

"Vishala" means big. The joined state kept the name Mysore. The capital moved from Mysore city to Bangalore.

ZappySo people from Dharwad and Bidar woke up as citizens of "Mysore"?

Prof. BoltExactly. Alur Venkata Rao called it "the now wrongly named State".

EP 5

Who wanted to keep "Mysore", and why

Fear 1: money

Old Mysore leaders feared the poorer northern regions would drain the richer Mysore region (Deccan Herald).

Fear 2: power

There was an unspoken worry that a united state would shift political power between communities.

Fear 3: pride

Respect for the Wadiyars and the "model state" made "Mysore" hard to let go. Some even wanted two states instead of one.

Prof. BoltHere's the twist, Zappy. Devaraj Urs, the Chief Minister who finally changed the name, said he had once opposed it himself.

EP 6

1972–73: Mysore becomes Karnataka

27 Jul 1972: Assembly votes yes, unanimously→1973: both Houses of Parliament agree→Mysore State (Alteration of Name) Act, 1973→1 Nov 1973: "Karnataka"

Why Urs changed his mind

"The majority opinion has to be accepted," Urs said. Activists like Vatal Nagaraj, A.J. Doddameti, K.H. Patil and Gopal Gowda had pushed for years.

HURRAY!

Celebrations were held across the state, especially at Hampi, the old Vijayanagara capital, where Alur Venkata Rao had begun his work.

17 years

From unification (1956) to the new name (1973).

THE PRESENT71 years on

EP 7

Rajyotsava 2026: 71 years, 71 awards

The awards

71

Rajyotsava awards announced on 30 October 2026, one for each year. Each winner gets ₹5 lakh and a 25-gram gold medal.

Who won

Yakshagana artiste Gopala Ganiga, writer Dr Indira Hegde, former Advocate General B.V. Acharya and more. Twelve are women.

A full circle

Vatal Nagaraj, one of the men who fought for the name "Karnataka" in 1972, was considered for an award this year but declined it.

EP 8

The renaming never really stopped

1 Nov 2014

Twelve cities got Kannada names. Bangalore became Bengaluru, Mysore became Mysuru, Belgaum became Belagavi.

2019

"Hyderabad-Karnataka" became Kalyana Karnataka: 7 districts, 1.13 crore people (2011 Census).

Nov 2021

"Mumbai-Karnataka" became Kittur Karnataka: Belagavi, Dharwad, Vijayapura and four more districts.

Every rename has the same idea: drop the name of an old ruler or an old province, and use a name from the land's own history.

THE DEEP DIVEThe research behind the headlines

EP 10

Where does the word "Karnataka" come from?

Very old

The oldest known mention is in the Mahabharata, about soldiers who came to fight at Kurukshetra (Prof. Purushottam Bilimale, in The Hindu).

Three meanings people suggest

  • Karu + nadu = high land
  • Kari nadu = land of black (cotton) soil
  • Kari = elephant

Scholars still argue. No single answer.

Used for centuries

Vijayanagara inscriptions say "Karnataka Desha". Kuvempu used "Karnataka" in his 1924 song Jaya Bharata Jananiya Tanujate, made the state anthem in 2004.

Prof. BoltSo "Mysore" was the newer name, the name of one kingdom. "Karnataka" was the name of the whole land, long before the British came.

So about 60% of the new state's land came from outside the old kingdom (our arithmetic). Yet it kept the kingdom's name.

EP 12

How does a state change its name?

State Assembly passes a resolution→Home Ministry gets "no objection" from Railways, Posts, Survey of India, Census→President recommends a Bill→State legislature gives its views→Parliament passes it by simple majority

The rule book

Article 3 of the Constitution gives Parliament the power. Article 4 says it is not a constitutional amendment, so a simple majority (more than half of those voting) is enough.

The catch

Parliament is not bound by the state's views. It can also say no. West Bengal's "Bangla" request (2018) is still waiting.

EP 14

The unfinished business

The Belagavi border

In 1957, Maharashtra claimed 814 villages and about 6.7 lakh people. The Mahajan Commission (report 1967) suggested swapping 264 places to Maharashtra and 247 to Karnataka. It was never carried out. Maharashtra went to the Supreme Court in 2004; the case is still open.

Help for the north

In 2012, the 98th Constitutional Amendment added Article 371J for Kalyana Karnataka: a development board, plus seats reserved for local people in colleges and state government jobs.

Prof. BoltOne name joined the map in 1973. Joining the incomes is taking much longer.

THE FUTUREProf. Bolt's verdict, and Zappy's plan

EP 15

Prof. Bolt's verdict (opinion)

FAIR!

The rename was fair. Most of the land came from outside old Mysore. A name for the whole land made everyone an equal owner.

PROUD!

Mysuru kept its glory. Dasara, the Palace and the Wadiyar legacy are still the state's pride. Mysuru did not shrink when the state's name grew.

TO DO!

The real test is the money gap. Bengaluru Urban earns five times Yadgir per person. The 1956 fear was about this; it is still the job to finish.

EP 16

So what should Zappy do?

Know your own place

  • Visit Hampi once; that's where the unification dream began.
  • Ask grandparents what they called the state in 1960. It's living history.

For daily life

  • Use the official names (Bengaluru, Mysuru, Belagavi) on forms and addresses.
  • From Kalyana Karnataka? Check the 371J local quota for college seats and state jobs.

ZappySo I can love Mysuru and Karnataka at the same time?

Prof. BoltThat was the whole point of 1973, Zappy.
