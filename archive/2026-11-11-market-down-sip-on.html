The vegetable-market trick

ZAPPYWhen tomatoes cost ₹100 a basket, ₹500 buys five. At ₹50, the same money buys ten. Fund units work similarly.
PROF. BOLTA lower NAV buys more units for the same instalment; it does not mean the investment is automatically cheap or safe.

EP 03

Two very different decisions

ZAPPYStopping future purchases is different from selling units already owned. One stops fresh investment; the other crystallises a sale at the prevailing NAV.
PROF. BOLTAsk whether your goal, cash flow or risk capacity changed—not merely the colour of today's chart.
Ask whether your goal, cash flow or risk capacity changed—not merely the colour of today's chart.
EP 04

The three-month experiment

ZAPPYImagine ₹5,000 invested at NAV ₹50, ₹40 and ₹25. You collect 100 + 125 + 200 = 425 units.
PROF. BOLT₹15,000 ÷ 425 = ₹35.29 average acquisition cost per unit. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.
₹5,000 / ₹50 = 100 units · ₹5,000 / ₹40 = 125 · ₹5,000 / ₹25 = 200
₹15,000 ÷ 425 = ₹35.

The twist nobody advertises

ZAPPYIf the NAV later stands at ₹30, those 425 units are worth ₹12,750. You are still down ₹2,250.
PROF. BOLTBuying more at lower prices does not protect you from a prolonged decline.

What if the market only rises?

THE DEEP DIVE — FOLLOW THE MONEY
EP 07

THE DEEP DIVE: What is NAV?

ZAPPYNAV is the fund's net assets divided by units outstanding. It is a unit price, not a stock's valuation ratio.
PROF. BOLTA ₹10 NAV fund is not inherently cheaper than a ₹100 NAV fund. Look at portfolio, mandate, costs and risk.
A ₹10 NAV fund is not inherently cheaper than a ₹100 NAV fund.
EP 08

THE DEEP DIVE: Where is the risk?

ZAPPYEquity funds can fall sharply; sector or small-cap funds may swing more than diversified funds. A SIP does not diversify a concentrated scheme.
PROF. BOLTCheck the scheme's Riskometer, investment horizon and whether you can tolerate a substantial temporary loss.
Check the scheme's Riskometer, investment horizon and whether you can tolerate a substantial temporary loss.
EP 09

THE DEEP DIVE: Your emergency fund

ZAPPYIf a salary disruption forces you to redeem during a crash, regular investing will not rescue the plan.
PROF. BOLTKeep near-term expenses and emergency reserves outside volatile equity; match the product to the time you need the money.
Keep near-term expenses and emergency reserves outside volatile equity; match the product to the time you need the money.

THE DEEP DIVE: The instalment clock

THE DEEP DIVE: What the educators say
