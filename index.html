7 NUMBERSThat explain India's love affair with gold

EP 2

Number 1: 25,000 tonnes

The gold in our homes

~25,000 t

That's Jefferies' estimate of gold held by Indian households (March 2026). UBS puts it at about 28,000 tonnes if you add temples: roughly 14% of all the gold above ground in the world.

What is it worth?

~₹350 lakh crore

25,000 tonnes at the 3 November price (our arithmetic). UBS says the stock is worth more than India's whole GDP for a year (all the goods and services the country makes).

ZappySo our lockers and almirahs hold a mountain?

Prof. BoltA sleeping mountain. Mostly jewellery, passed from mothers to daughters, rarely sold.

EP 3

Number 2: 880 tonnes

The RBI's gold

880.5 t

Gold held by the Reserve Bank of India on 31 March 2026. It was 16.7% of India's foreign-exchange reserves, up from 13.9% in September 2025.

Homes vs the central bank

~28×

Households hold about 28 times the RBI's gold (our arithmetic: 25,000 ÷ 880). An Assocham report says India's household gold is bigger than the top 10 central banks' gold put together.

Prof. BoltIn most countries, the government holds the big gold pile. In India, families do.

EP 4

Number 3: 1.7 tonnes (Karnataka's own gold)

Hutti Gold Mines, Raichur

1,692 kg

Gold dug up by Karnataka's Hutti Gold Mines in 2025-26, 99.5% of its target, with a profit of ₹844 crore. It is the only public-sector company that mines gold directly.

Now compare

Indians bought 162.5 tonnes of gold in just July–September 2026 (World Gold Council). Hutti's whole year is about 1% of that one quarter (our arithmetic).

TINY!

ZappySo where does all our gold come from?

Prof. BoltShips and planes. Almost all of it is imported, and paid for in dollars.

EP 5

Number 4: 15% import duty

13 May 2026

6%→15%

The government raised the import duty (a tax at the border) on gold and silver, to cut imports and support the rupee. The World Gold Council called it the steepest rise on record.

Why a government cares

We buy gold abroad→We pay in dollars→More dollars leave India→The rupee weakens

What happened next

Official imports in July–September fell to 64 tonnes, down 70% from a year earlier (WGC).

Prof. BoltBut Indians didn't stop wanting gold. Some of it just found another door.

EP 6

Number 5: 50 tonnes through the back door

Unofficial gold, July–Sept 2026

3 t→~50 t

The WGC estimates about 50 tonnes came in unofficially (smuggled, with no duty paid), against about 3 tonnes a year earlier.

PSST!

Duty plus GST now add up to 18.45%. Dealers told Reuters (via Kitco, June 2026) that a smuggled 1 kg bar earns a margin of over ₹25 lakh. Some expect smuggling to top 100 tonnes in 2026.

"No-bill" gold sold below the market rate may be smuggled. It is illegal, has no hallmark guarantee, and you have no protection if it is impure.
EP 7

Number 6: ₹18.6 lakh crore of gold loans

Gold as a cash machine

₹18.6 lakh cr

Gold loans outstanding in March 2026 (Motilal Oswal): up about 50% in a year and nearly 4× in five years. By September, estimates put it near ₹20 lakh crore.

How a gold loan works

Pledge your jewellery→Bank lends up to 75–85% of its value→Repay with interest→Get your gold back

The share it lends is called "LTV" (loan-to-value).

A southern habit

Five southern states, including Karnataka, hold almost 75% of India's gold loans (LoansJagat, May 2026).

Prof. BoltWhen gold rises, the same necklace can raise more money. That is why loans jumped.

New rule

Since July 2025, the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) also hallmarks 9 carat gold (at least 37.5% gold), so cheaper jewellery can carry an official purity stamp.

What shops report

With gold near ₹1.5 lakh in October, jewellers saw buyers ask for lighter designs, lower carats and old-gold exchange (Business Today, 3 Oct 2026).

Lower carat = less gold, not fake gold. It's cheaper to wear, but you get less back per gram when you sell.

EP 11

Gold loan maths: safe, until it isn't

A worked example (our arithmetic)

10 g of 22K jewellery = ₹1,27,910.

  • At 75% LTV you could borrow about ₹95,900. If gold falls 25%, the necklace is worth only the loan.
  • At 55% LTV (the average for banks, per Motilal Oswal) you'd borrow about ₹70,350. Gold must fall about 45% before the loan is uncovered.

RBI's view (Sept 2026)

Governor Sanjay Malhotra said the RBI sees no overheating: bad loans are only about 0.4–0.5%, and banks lend well below the limits.

Motilal Oswal found 82% of new gold loans in 2025 were repeat or top-up loans, up from 76% in 2022. Borrowing again and again against the same gold can become a debt trap.
EP 12

Why the mountain won't move

The scheme that tried

31 t

The Gold Monetisation Scheme (2015) paid interest if you deposited idle gold in a bank. In nine years it collected only about 31 tonnes. Its long-term parts were stopped in March 2025.

What the experts say

  • Jefferies (Sept 2026): only about 15% of household gold is "monetised" (put to work, mostly via loans). A 10% rise in gold adds about $400 billion to household wealth.
  • UBS (Jan 2026): households rarely sell, so this wealth does little for everyday spending.
  • Assocham (Apr 2026): moving 2% of household gold into financial forms each year could greatly boost growth.

ZappyWhy won't people put it in a bank?

Prof. BoltA bank melts it. A mother's bangle isn't just 20 grams; it's a memory. Feelings beat interest rates.

EP 13

The Karnataka angle: the old gold state

Kolar Gold Fields (KGF)

~800 t

Gold mined at KGF from 1880 to 2001. Its Champion Reef shaft went over 3.2 km deep. In 1902 KGF became the first place in India to get hydroelectric power, from Shivanasamudra on the Kaveri.

Why it closed

45 g of gold per tonne of rock→Under 5 g→Costs above gold value→Shut on 1 March 2001

Second life?

The old waste heaps (tailings) hold about 24,279 kg of gold, with about 54% recoverable: roughly 13 tonnes, or eight years of Hutti output (our arithmetic). In June 2026 the Geological Survey of India found new gold-bearing rock near KGF.

THE VERDICTProf. Bolt's opinion, and Zappy's plan

EP 14

Prof. Bolt's verdict (opinion)

SMART!

Indian families weren't foolish. Gold has protected savings when the rupee fell, and it now backs ₹18 lakh crore of easy loans. The locker has been a quiet bank.

BUT...

A sleeping mountain costs the country. We pay dollars for gold we rarely use, high duty brings back smuggling, and lighter carats mean we pay more for less metal.

MY TAKE

Keep the tradition, change the tool. Buy jewellery to wear and gift. For savings, choose regulated paper gold. Treat gold loans as emergency help, not a habit.

EP 15

So what should Zappy do?

At the jeweller

  • Look for the BIS hallmark, the carat mark and the 6-character HUID code on every piece.
  • Ask for making charges and the buy-back rule in writing.
  • Always take a GST bill. Skip "no-bill" discounts.

For saving, not wearing

  • Gold ETFs are regulated by SEBI and have no making charges. Indians put ₹4,451 crore into them in September 2026 alone.
  • Keep gold a small part of your savings. Buy in steps, not all on one day.

If you ever need a gold loan

  • Borrow well below the limit, so a price fall doesn't trigger an auction.
  • Prefer a bank, compare interest rates, and repay before taking another.
